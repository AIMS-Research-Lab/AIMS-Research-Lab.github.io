How to apply
What should I send?

Email a short note on why you're interested in the lab, your CV, transcripts, and links to any code, papers or projects. Put the position in the subject line, e.g. "PhD application: [Your Name]".

Do I need prior machine learning experience?

Not necessarily. Strong fundamentals in engineering, physics or mathematics, and a willingness to learn, matter more than any specific tool.

When are applications reviewed?

PhD admissions follow [University Name]'s admission cycle. Postdoc and internship enquiries are reviewed on a rolling basis.

I didn't hear back. What now?

We read every message, but volume can be high. If your profile is a match for a current opening, we'll be in touch within a few weeks.
