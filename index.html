We build machine intelligence that understands the physical world, to simulate, explain and design the materials and systems of tomorrow.

Who we are

We work where machine learning meets mechanics, physics and materials. Our models don't just fit data. They respect the principles that govern it, generalise beyond it, and help us create things that have never been built before.

Based at [University Name], AIMS brings together researchers from engineering, applied mathematics and computer science. We work on simulation, prediction and design, and we care about rigour, open science and real-world impact.

Our research
Research themes

Four directions,
one ambition.

Explore research
01

Learning with the laws of nature

Neural models trained on governing principles as well as observations, so they stay faithful where data is scarce.
02

Learning entire solution spaces

Fast surrogates that map conditions to full physical responses, across geometries and resolutions.
03

Architected matter

Materials whose behaviour comes from geometry. They respond to force in ways ordinary solids cannot.
04

Intelligent design loops

Closing the gap between simulation, learning and fabrication, from prediction to inverse design.
Our approach

Principles first. Then data. Then design.

01

Start from physics

We encode what is already known (conservation, symmetry, constitutive behaviour) directly into how models learn.

02

Learn what generalises

We build models that transfer across scales, shapes and conditions, not ones that memorise a single scenario.

03

Design what's next

Fast, reliable predictions let us search vast design spaces and propose structures worth building and testing.
